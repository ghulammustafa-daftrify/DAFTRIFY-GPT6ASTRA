The problem

Small differences become expensive problems.

Document-heavy work rarely fails because one file is impossible to read. It fails when information is scattered across files, versions disagree, evidence is missing, or nobody has time to check the whole set.

Name mismatchDifferent spellings or formats across records.
Date mismatchDates that do not reconcile between sources.
Amount mismatchFigures that require source-level checking.
Missing evidenceA required item is absent or unclear.
Wrong versionMultiple drafts make the working set ambiguous.
Manual reworkStaff spend time checking instead of moving work forward.

Scope & FAQ

Clear boundaries, clear output.

Is DAFTRIFY software or a service?

DAFTRIFY is positioned as an outsourced document-operations service. Internal tooling and automation can support the workflow, but the customer-facing offer is the checked working output.

Do you guarantee approvals or professional outcomes?

No. DAFTRIFY organizes and checks document work. It does not guarantee an approval, decision, legal outcome, financial result or other third-party decision.

Are the examples on this website real client cases?

No. The examples on this site are explicitly synthetic demonstrations. They should not be interpreted as client history, testimonials, approval rates or performance claims.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is scoped from the document type, volume, source quality, complexity and required output. A quote is provided after the workflow is understood.

Can a workflow outside the listed industries be handled?

Potentially. The listed categories communicate the type of work the desk is designed for, but actual fit is determined from the documents and desired output.

What does human verification mean?

It means flagged or relevant information is reviewed against the source before the working package is returned. It is not a guarantee that a third party will accept the documents.
