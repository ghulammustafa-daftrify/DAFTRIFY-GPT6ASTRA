01 / THE PROBLEM

Documents become operational risk when nobody owns the middle.

A file arrives. Another follows. Names shift. Dates drift. Amounts disagree. Evidence lives in different places. The work between receiving documents and trusting them is where DAFTRIFY operates.

The goal is not to make more documents. It is to make the document situation clearer.
SCATTERED

Files, tables, scans and statements arrive in different shapes.

INCONSISTENT

Related records can contain small differences with large consequences.

UNVERIFIED

Extracted information still needs to be checked against its source.

UNREADY

A working package should be organized before someone relies on it.

06 / OPERATING STANDARD

How the desk works.

The quality bar is operational, not decorative. These principles govern what gets surfaced, what gets verified and what DAFTRIFY refuses to pretend it knows.

01

SOURCE FIRST.

The source record comes before assumptions.

02

EXCEPTIONS ARE VISIBLE.

Differences and unresolved items should be surfaced, not hidden.

03

VERIFY AGAINST THE RECORD.

Important findings are checked against the underlying document or source.

04

NO INVENTED FACTS.

Missing information is not filled with guesses.

05

NO FAKE PROOF.

Simulated work is clearly labeled and never presented as client history.

06

OUTPUT SHOULD BE CLEARER THAN INPUT.

The final working package should make the document situation easier to understand and act on.
