THE OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Make the messy middle visible.

DAFTRIFY is built around a simple operational idea: document workflows should expose uncertainty instead of hiding it. Extraction helps move faster; reconciliation makes differences visible; human verification controls the final handoff.

Source-aware

Important outputs stay traceable to the underlying source material.

Exception-first

Conflicts and missing information are surfaced for review.

Human-in-the-loop

Automation supports the workflow; it does not replace the reviewer.

Workflow-specific

Processes are scoped around the actual document set.

FAQ / LIMITATIONS

Clear scope. No black box.

Is DAFTRIFY a document-management SaaS?

No. It is positioned as an outsourced document-operations layer around a defined workflow.

Does DAFTRIFY make decisions for my business?

No. It can organize information and flag exceptions, while substantive professional decisions remain with the appropriate human professional or client.

What happens when two documents disagree?

The disagreement is treated as an exception. Conflicting source values can be preserved and presented for review rather than silently choosing one.

Do you use real client data in the website demo?

No. The example is synthetic and exists only to demonstrate the workflow concept.

How is pricing determined?

Engagements are scope-based. Volume, complexity, frequency, turnaround, reconciliation depth, and output requirements affect the quote.
