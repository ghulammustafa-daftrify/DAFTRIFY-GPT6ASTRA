06 / PRINCIPLES

Source first.
Always.

01

EXCEPTIONS ARE VISIBLE.

Differences should be surfaced as differences, not quietly rewritten into a cleaner-looking record.

02

VERIFY AGAINST THE RECORD.

Extracted information and confirmed information are not the same thing. The source remains the reference.

03

NO INVENTED FACTS.

When a detail is missing, uncertain or outside scope, the working package should say so.

04

THE OUTPUT SHOULD BE CLEARER THAN THE INPUT.

Organization is part of the service. The finished package should make the next professional review easier.
